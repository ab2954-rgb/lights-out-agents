# POL-3 Model Risk and Autonomy Policy
AI agents operating finance processes are subject to model risk management aligned with SR 11-7 and to DORA operational resilience requirements.
Autonomy levels range from A0 (observe) to A4 (lights-out) and are promoted only on evidence from the evaluation harness: accuracy, sample size, and incident rate thresholds.
An incident rate breach at the current level triggers automatic demotion by one level and a review by the model risk committee.
Human oversight is exercised through interrupts in the agent workflow and is evidenced in the Evidence Ledger.
