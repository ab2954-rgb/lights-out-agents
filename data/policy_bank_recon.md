# POL-1 Bank Reconciliation Policy
Bank accounts are reconciled to the general ledger monthly, no later than business day 3 of the close.
Reconciling items must be classified as timing, error, or unrecorded transaction, with an owner and an ageing date.
Unrecorded bank charges below 100.00 in reporting currency may be posted by the reconciliation agent to account 6150-BankCharges without pre-approval when the autonomy level is A2 or higher.
Any reconciling item above 100000.00 must be approved by the regional controller before posting.
Items ageing more than 60 days are escalated to the Controller and reported in the close scorecard.
# POL-1 Evidence
Every automated match, adjustment, or escalation is recorded in the Evidence Ledger with the control ID it evidences (SOX-R2R-03, SOX-R2R-05).
