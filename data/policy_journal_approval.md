# POL-2 Journal Entry Approval Policy
Manual and agent-initiated journal entries require a preparer and an approver who are different identities (segregation of duties).
Journals below 5000.00 prepared by an approved agent at autonomy level A2 or above are auto-approved and sampled at 10 percent for review.
Journals between 5000.00 and 100000.00 require approval by the local finance manager.
Journals above 100000.00 require approval by the regional controller and are flagged in the close scorecard.
Reversing entries must reference the original journal id.
